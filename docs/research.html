Scientific rationale and limits

Geothermal-indicative faults are a structure problem.

The target is not a vent pixel. It is a fault-like structure that may control permeability and geothermal circulation, inferred from geophysical and geomorphic signatures while avoiding already mapped catalogue support.

Physical mechanism

In the Great Basin setting, a blind or poorly exposed fault can juxtapose magnetic, gravity, conductivity, and basement-depth domains. A fault can also provide a damage zone or permeability pathway without a preserved surface scarp. H82-B therefore asks whether signed curvature disagreement across independent layers is more informative than a single bright anomaly.

The detector is deliberately conservative: four layers are smoothed at 2 px, their gradient magnitudes and signed Gaussian-Laplacian responses are robust-normalized, and the score is gated toward weak detrended-elevation curvature. The emission then keeps the highest field values outside a 3-pixel visible-catalogue collar.

Metric decision theory

The organizer's problem page defines a triangular kernel k(d)=max(1-d/R,0) with R=300 m and a distance-weighted Tversky index with alpha=0.2 and beta=0.8. The evaluator computes TP as the maximum weighted prediction around each truth pixel, FP as prediction mass discounted by distance to truth, and FN as the remaining truth credit.

That asymmetry makes “more dots” an unsafe default. A candidate must allocate mass where it is likely to add new 300 m coverage, not simply reproduce every visible line.

Holdout construction

  1. Use the finite sample footprint and the positive catalogue raster.
  2. Label whole eight-connected catalogue components; select components wholly inside each of four spatial quadrants.
  3. Withhold approximately one fifth of each quadrant's eligible components. Keep all other catalogue pixels visible.
  4. Remove a 3-pixel collar around withheld components from the visible catalogue feature context. H82-B itself is catalogue-independent, but this rule prevents future catalogue features from seeing withheld traces.
  5. Mask visible catalogue pixels exactly in the scored domain; do not score predictions on them.
  6. Emit a matched budget, compute official metric terms, pool by 200-pixel physical blocks, and bootstrap those blocks for a 95% interval.

This is a catalogue hide-and-recover instrument. It cannot measure the private expert-labelled test set and therefore cannot certify leaderboard performance.

Why a negative result is useful

H82-B's HOLDOUT-DTI is modest and its interval is wide relative to the private competition problem. It tells us not to promote the file merely because it is novel and format-valid. The saved surface, canaries, metrics, and run card are reusable evidence for a future selector or for a better hypothesis without spending a slot on this result.
