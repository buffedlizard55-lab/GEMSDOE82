6. Forensic Irregularities Log, Limitations & Next Steps

  • IR-82-01 (Critical — Fixed in GEMSDOE82): Line-by-line inspection of GEMSDOE77/scripts/h85_edti_pipeline.py revealed that H85-EDTI (which reported 0.2966 locally) trained its trees across all 4 quadrants simultaneously (including the test quadrant), filtered negatives using d_cat > 3.0 from the full catalogue (including withheld hidden faults), passed the test fold's ground-truth pixel count k_fold = f["truth"].sum() into the emitter, and emitted 201,260 positive pixels in production vs. ~37,429 on holdout. H82-CSAM eliminates all three defects via strict Leave-One-Quadrant-Out (LOQO) isolation and fixed discrete dot budgets (41,200 / 37,654).
  • IR-82-02 (High — Fixed in GEMSDOE82): Enforcing a 3.0-px overlap cap against prior production rasters inside a hide-and-recover holdout artificially increases HOLDOUT-DTI by +0.0239 (0.1658 → 0.1897) because prior production rasters masked out the full catalogue (`cat = visible | hidden`), leaving empty 2–3 px holes along `hidden` test faults. We report strictly uncapped fold-clean HOLDOUT-DTI (0.165778 pooled) as our headline metric.
  • IR-82-03 (Medium — Handled): 19 of the 188 prior rasters (dense_witness.tif and 18 continuous/lattice rasters) cover 98.9%–100% of the footprint when dilated by 3.0 px; per vendor/gems57/uniqueness.py, dense surfaces are gated by Spearman $\rho \le 0.90$ (max = 0.2521) while all 168 sparse dot rasters are gated by both $\rho \le 0.90$ (max = 0.1687) and 3.0-px overlap $\le 0.70$ (max = 0.6100).
  • Limitations & Next Steps: (1) Up-dip back-projection in H82-CSAM uses a uniform 250 m ($2.5\text{ px}$) horizontal offset; future work should invert spatially varying fault dip angles $\delta(x) \in [35^\circ, 70^\circ]$ from curie-depth and 3D gravity gradients. (2) Upload gems82-h82-csam-dinkelbach-41200-20261021-zeros.tif to DrivenData to record its ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED live score in registry/live_scores.csv.
